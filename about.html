About

Now

Principal Engineer at realfast, wrangling AI. I lead a team that builds agents and an AI harness that carries work from analysis through development and QA.

Shovel Company

2021–2024

I co-founded Shovel Company and took our products from idea to prototype to delivery. The main one was Creole Network, a peer-to-peer, user-controlled private data network. Any two people can connect over an end-to-end encrypted channel using keys they own, and their social graph comes with them instead of living inside one app. I gave a talk on it at LoFi, the local-first meetup, in 2024.

On top of it we built Rolodex, a private contact book that 10 communities used as their members directory, with 400 sign-ups. We also built Odet, a service registry and developer platform for tracking DORA metrics.

We couldn’t find a sustainable product-market fit.

Gojek

2016–2021

I was on the team that built and scaled Gojek’s infrastructure, and went from product engineer to engineering manager for observability, leading a team of ten. Some of what we built:

  • Ziggurat, a stream-processing framework that cut the time to ship a Kafka app from 40 hours to 30 minutes. Over 200 services used it.
  • Lens, our multi-tenant monitoring on Cortex, which replaced Datadog and InfluxDB and took in about 2M samples a second. Grafana Labs wrote a case study about it.
  • Uptime tracking for every public API, from 2M events a minute, and a platform for SLOs and error budgets.
  • One deployment platform across countries, clusters and clouds, which took deploy success from 65% to 90%.

Early on, I was SRE and on call for over 100 services. I also taught Gojek’s yearly DevOps bootcamp, and spoke at DevOpsDays in 2016 and 2017.

Where I started

2015

After a B.Tech at IIT Jodhpur, I joined C42 Engineering. My first project was the service that brought Google Maps into Gojek’s app. I’ve been writing since 2015.
